Chapter I · Orientation

Welcome to Paradise (Terms and Conditions Apply)

Castaway Island is a tiny, animated 3D world inhabited by one spectacularly persevering gentleman. The tide comes and goes, the palm sways, and very little goes according to plan.

Part living screensaver, part miniature theatre, it stages wordless physical comedy in short scenes approximately every thirty seconds. No score, no quest log, and no urgent responsibility to save a man from his own engineering.

Official island motto: There is always tomorrow.

Chapter IV · Selected Misadventures

The Castaway's Field Journal

Each entry documents an original scene in the current catalog. Other routines and future chapters may appear in the expedition's plans; this is a field guide, not a promise that every fanciful idea has already escaped its notebook.

The Portable Shade

A splendid leaf canopy is engineered for personal comfort. The breeze moves the shadow behind its owner, who pursues it in a slow and increasingly formal circle. The actual tree has been providing shade throughout.

Nature has already invented several of your inventions.

Diplomatic Relations

A crab approaches. Our hero presents a leaf-gloved hand. The crab accepts the glove, declines the handshake, and leaves the negotiations wearing its prize.

Never offer a crab anything you need back.

The Perfect Timepiece

After arranging pebbles around a driftwood sundial, the scientist records the hour. A palm frond shifts; the shadow changes. He concludes the experiment with a nap.

Time is relative, especially beneath a tree.

Return to Sender

A desperate letter is folded, bottled, corked, and consigned to the ocean. The current returns it; the crab completes the delivery.

Your correspondence has been received.

The Magnificent Pier

One final plank completes the engineering masterpiece. The sea retreats out of reach. The castaway erects a sign reading OPEN TOMORROW.

Infrastructure should occasionally consult the sea.

Chapter V · Scheduling Department

The Nature of Island Time

An original vignette lasts thirty seconds and ordinarily follows six causal beats. If the joke seems slow, that is usually because it is preparing to become very fast indeed.

0–6sCalm
6–12sPreparation
12–19sAttempt
19–23sReversal
23–28sAftermath
28–30sRecovery

The story director discourages immediate repetition and remembers certain events. A crab, for example, must first become an acquaintance before acquiring a second career in the postal service. Where supported, this story history is stored in your browser.

Standing Orders from the Island

  1. Every problem deserves an overengineered solution.
  2. Every solution deserves a moment of pride.
  3. Every moment of pride is vulnerable to the tide.
  4. No misfortune is complete until the beard has stopped moving.
  5. Tomorrow is another opportunity for the ocean to disagree.

Chapter VI · The Local Climate

Natural History & Sound

The island is made from stylized three-dimensional shapes, animated props, water, shadows, and rather expressive miniature acting. Stillness matters. A lingering glance often does more work than a spectacular fall.

Opt-in, procedurally produced ambience and brief comic effects accompany the surf. It is designed to feel like a peaceful tropical day accidentally interrupted by a determined person's ambitions.

Some storytelling, staging, animation, and accessibility refinements remain in development. The living island, rather than this printed account, is the final authority on what is presently playable.

Chapter VII · Correspondence

Questions from Concerned Relatives

Can he escape?

He is working on it. He has become rather attached to the view.

Is there a winning condition?

Not at present. Watching is the point; the castaway supplies the ambition.

Does the story end?

Scenes recur and vary. Larger arcs remain in development; no completed long-form finale is promised.

Is this the old Johnny Castaway screensaver?

No. This is an independent original work inspired by the tradition of wordless screensaver comedy. Its story events, character, geometry, and implementation are original. It is not affiliated with the creators or rights holders of Johnny Castaway.

Is there anything I can do to help?

You may visit the island, inspect the source repository, or propose an original scene through the issue tracker. The castaway would prefer a boat.

Chapter VIII · Production Office

Publication Notes

Castaway Island is an independent browser-based Three.js diorama built with Vite+ and published on GitHub Pages. For installation, testing, architecture, and contribution instructions, consult the repository README. For the running research and animation bible, see issue #1.

This field guide describes five original catalog vignettes and the present interaction model; it deliberately distinguishes working behavior from future ideas. No historical artwork, dialogue, or proprietary scene assets are reproduced.
